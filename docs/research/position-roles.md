# Draft position roles

The wheel previously admitted partial matrix compatibility (for example CM → CAM), while cards and pitch placement required explicit roles. Now selection, cards, placement and swaps use the same advertised roles. Same-flank game equivalents are RB/RWB, LB/LWB, RM/RW, LM/LW and ST/CF. No opposite-flank, goalkeeper or unrelated central roles are added automatically.

Two player-specific additions apply only to Zenit records starting in 2017–2018:

- Yuriy Zhirkov: left-back and left midfield. Official match reports place him in defence on 25 January 2018 and midfield on 14 April 2018.
  - https://fc-zenit.ru/news/2018-01-25-zenit-slaviya-driussi-i-zabolotnyy-poyavyatsya-s-pervykh-minut.htm
  - https://fc-zenit.ru/news/2018-04-14-zenit-anzhi-driussi-i-kranevitter-poyavyatsya-s-pervykh-minut.htm
- Sebastián Driussi: left wing and striker. Official lineups place him in attack in January/April 2018; Sergei Semak describes his left-flank and central attacking role on 31 March 2019.
  - https://fc-zenit.ru/news/2019-03-31-sergey-semak-v-chempionate-rossii-net-matchey-v-kotorykh-s-legkostyu-mozhno-predskazat-rezultat.htm

These are narrowly scoped game-role interpretations of the reports, not a claim that every player has played all adjacent positions. Additions appear in API cards and are stored with the chosen squad slot. Ratings and source archive data are preserved.
